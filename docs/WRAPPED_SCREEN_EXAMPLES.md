## Responsive Behavior

**Small Phones (< 375px):**
- Font sizes scale down 10%
- Padding reduced to 16px
- Cards stack better

**Large Phones (> 414px):**
- Font sizes scale up 5%
- More breathing room
- Larger touch targets

**Tablets:**
- Center content with max width
- Larger icons and text
- Maintain aspect ratios

## Key Takeaways

✅ **Immersive**: Full-screen pages demand attention
✅ **Delightful**: Smooth animations feel premium
✅ **Personal**: Uses member's name and real data
✅ **Flexible**: Conditional pages adapt to user activity
✅ **Engaging**: Swipe interaction feels natural
✅ **Celebratory**: Positive messaging throughout
✅ **Shareable**: Visual format perfect for screenshots

This creates an experience that makes members feel **valued** and **celebrated** for their contributions!
