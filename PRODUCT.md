# Product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Parkroad Fellowship church members using the app for faith activities such as missions, giving, prayer requests, FAQs, announcements, and community connection. The primary audience is congregants, and the product is used as part of everyday faith life when they want to stay connected to the fellowship from wherever they are.

## Product Purpose

PRF Missions is the all-in-one digital hub for Parkroad Fellowship. It exists to make participation in fellowship life easier by helping members discover and join missions, give, submit prayer requests, access FAQs, and stay informed through announcements. Success looks like stronger participation, more giving, and deeper community connection.

## Positioning

A purpose-built fellowship hub for Parkroad Fellowship, not a generic church app template or a corporate SaaS product.

## Operating Context

Members use the app in everyday ministry and community contexts across mobile devices. Core workflows include exploring missions, participating in fellowship activities, giving, sending prayer requests, reading updates, and staying connected. The product is maintained by FROG Technologies in partnership with Parkroad Fellowship and is delivered as a Flutter-based mobile application with support for iOS, Android, and web-facing project structure.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include missions participation, giving, prayer requests, FAQs, announcements, and community connection. The product is built with Flutter and Dart, uses Firebase-backed services, and follows a modular feature-based architecture. The app should remain accessible, reliable, and welcoming for a broad congregation, including users who may prefer simple, low-friction interactions.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is PRF Missions and it should feel warm, grounded, purposeful, rooted in faith, mission-driven, and welcoming. The experience should feel like an extension of the fellowship rather than a templated or overly corporate product.

## Product Principles

- Intentionality: each interaction should feel purposeful rather than filler.
- Authenticity: the experience should feel like it belongs to PRF, not a template.
- Accessibility of action: reduce friction so participation feels effortless.
- Trust through consistency: create reliable, predictable experiences.
- Celebrate community: acknowledge milestones and shared journeys.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product should meet WCAG AA expectations where practical, with strong contrast, clear focus states, screen-reader support, and touch targets that work well for a broad congregation. Reduced motion and low-friction interactions should be considered where appropriate.
