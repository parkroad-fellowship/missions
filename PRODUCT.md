# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Parkroad Fellowship church members using the app for faith activities — missions, giving, prayer requests, FAQs, and community connection. The primary audience is congregants; there is no secondary audience. Members use this app in the context of their daily faith life, staying connected to the fellowship wherever they are.

## Product Purpose

PRF Missions is the all-in-one digital hub for Parkroad Fellowship. It exists to deepen engagement by making participation in fellowship life effortless — from going on missions and suggesting new ones, to giving, submitting prayer requests, and staying informed through announcements. Success looks like more missions completed, more giving, and stronger community bonds.

## Positioning

All-in-one fellowship hub — purpose-built for Parkroad Fellowship.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, purposeful. The app should feel like an extension of the fellowship itself: rooted in faith, driven by mission, and welcoming to every member.

## Anti-references

- Generic church app templates — PRF Missions is purpose-built, not off-the-shelf
- Corporate SaaS interfaces — no cold, enterprise-looking design
- Overly trendy design patterns — keep it timeless, not chasing fleeting aesthetics

## Design Principles

- **Intentionality**: Every interaction should feel purposeful, not filler
- **Authenticity**: The app should feel like it belongs to PRF, not a template
- **Accessibility of action**: Make participation effortless — reduce friction at every step
- **Trust through consistency**: Build trust through reliable, predictable experiences
- **Celebrate community**: Acknowledge milestones and individual journeys

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA standard. Follow Flutter defaults for contrast, focus indicators, and screen reader support. Consider reduced motion preferences and ensure reasonable touch targets for all age groups in the congregation.
